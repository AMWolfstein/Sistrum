## Phase 3 - Native component adoption (checklist items 4, 6, 7, 8)

> Note: the Frosted parts of this phase are historical. Frosted was removed in Phase 15 (decision 181).

Decisions 22-50 are in `TOR006/decisions.md`. Summary:

- Item 4 (wrapper): `MornyeGlass` keeps its API. Liquid = `MornyeLiquidBackdrop` (package
  `LiquidGlassLens`) in a `LiquidGlassBatch` (default `separatePass`). Frosted =
  `LiquidGlassLite` (preset sigma 3). None = plain fill, no backdrop read, no batch.
  Liquid without a lens falls back to Lite (sigma 2). `BackdropFilter.grouped` and the blur
  table are removed.
- Item 6 (bottom bar, tab row, press): the tab row keeps `MornyeTabBar`'s API. Its selection is
  the package `LiquidGlassMotionPill` (glass mode only), and its capsule is the native lens or lite
  surface through `MornyeGlass`. `LiquidGlassTabBar`, `LiquidGlassMorph` and `LiquidGlassScaffold`
  were evaluated and not adopted, because the bottom bar's fold, side capsules and layout are
  owned by Sistrum (decisions 37-39). `MornyeGlassPress` is kept: `LiquidGlassTouch` only presses
  its own lens (decision 41). `BackdropGroup` is removed from the bottom bar.
- Item 7 (selection): `lib/widgets/mornye_selection_pill.dart` and
  `assets/shaders/mornye_selection_mask.frag` are deleted, with the `shaders:` entry in
  `pubspec.yaml`. One private `_SelectionRow` serves the segmented control and the tab bar. Liquid
  uses the native thumb. Frosted and None use an ordinary tinted capsule. Drag preview and the
  shader-masked foreground are removed (decisions 28-36). The shader-based tests and integration
  tests are replaced with native checks.
- Item 8 (rim, shadows, clipping): `_MornyeGlassRim` is removed. The native lens and
  `LiquidGlassLite` draw the rim with the same values (`mornyeRimSpecular`, `mornyeRimGlow`).
  The outer shadow and the clips are kept, because the native clip and shadow were not verified
  on a device (decision 27).
