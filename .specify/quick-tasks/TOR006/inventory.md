# TOR006 Phase 4 - Recursive migration inventory (plan 12.8)

- Task: TOR006 (record `.specify/quick-tasks/TOR006.md`). Phase 4 scope: everything outside the core
  chrome that still renders glass or blur on its own (audit items 5, 9, 12; plan sections 11 and 12).
- Method: every route, settings page, overlay and presenter in `TOR006/audit.md` section 3, plus every
  file that uses a glass wrapper, a `LiquidGlass*` type, `BackdropFilter`, `ImageFilter` or `ImageFiltered`
  (grep over `lib/`, Phase 4 tree). Sites the audit did not list are marked **added**.
- Mode shorthand: **L** Liquid (native lens in a batch, device-capable), **F** Frosted (`LiquidGlassLite`,
  preset sigma 3), **N** None (plain fill, no backdrop read, no batch). "Mode-aware" means the surface reads
  `mornyeGlassLevelProvider` or `mornyeLiquidGlassProvider` through `MornyeGlass` or `MornyeGlassPreset`,
  so mounted widgets follow a live change (`test/mornye_overlay_mode_test.dart`).
- Status words: **Replaced** (independent renderer removed, native or central wrapper), **Simplified** (same
  surface, clarity or per-site values removed), **Retained** (already central, or not glass; reason given),
  **Removed** (deleted).
- Presets (`lib/theme/mornye_glass_presets.dart`): Liquid sigma 2, Frosted sigma 3, None 0.

## F. Summary of counts (Phase 4 tree)

- Independent glass renderers remaining in `lib/`: **none that bypass the mode policy**. The remaining `BackdropFilter` is the desktop inspector preset (section C); the remaining `ImageFiltered` and `ImageFilter.blur` calls are artwork, lyric and preview effects (section E), and the liquid-only artist header blur.
- Replaced in Phase 4: desktop inspector sigma 60 to the preset (sigma 2 or 3), and the bottom bar batch disabled in None.
- Items flagged for device measurement or visual check: album non-immersive `SettledBlur` in N, artwork contrast readback, motion glow over video, desktop inspector legibility, artist header blur in L.
